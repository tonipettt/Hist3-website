The watawat stands as a visible sign of identity and belonging. It shows how symbols can carry stories, values, and pride within a community.

A community at work

This scene shows culture through shared action. Work, movement, and cooperation become part of how a community expresses who they are.

The sacred Landscape

Some places feel meaningful because of the stories and practices connected to them. The landscape becomes part of memory, belief, and identity.

Figure 3. A landscape connected to local meaning.

Culture continues when children learn, join, and take part in community life. Their presence shows how traditions are passed on in simple everyday moments.
